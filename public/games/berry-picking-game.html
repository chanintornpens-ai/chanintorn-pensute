The wild berry season

You are a rice farmer from northeast Thailand. A broker promises that two months picking wild berries in Finland will earn you 100,000 baht. So you take out a bank loan and mortgage your farmland — 140,000 baht of debt — to cover the broker's fee, airfare, visa and a medical check, before you even leave.

Once you reach the forest, the reality is: you are paid by the kilo, and the company deducts food, housing, car rental and fuel from your pay every week. Your passport is taken. You work 14–15 hours a day, no days off, for 10 weeks straight.

Goal: pick enough berries to repay the 140,000 baht debt and bring money home · Click the berry bushes on screen as fast as you can each week.
